How It Works

Create an Escrow Transaction

Buyer and Seller agree on terms and initiate the transaction on the platform.

Fund the Escrow Account

Buyer deposits the agreed amount into the secure escrow account.

Deliver Goods or Services

Seller fulfills their part by delivering the product or service.

Complete the Transaction

Buyer reviews and confirms that everything meets the agreed terms.

Release Payment

Funds are released to the seller once the buyer approves the delivery.
